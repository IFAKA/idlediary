# IdleDiary Social Launch Guide

## Readiness

IdleDiary is mobile-first. The launch demo and final smoke test should happen on a real phone, because the product depends on the phone camera, capture permissions, vertical layout, and the share/download flow.

Before posting, resolve these launch blockers:

- E2E test cleanup.
- Social preview image.
- Demo video.

## Demo Video Plan

Record the main demo on a real phone. Use a clean phone screen recording, not a desktop emulator.

Show the product in this order:

1. Open IdleDiary intro.
2. Tap `Start recording`.
3. Show the camera screen.
4. Record one 3-second clip.
5. Open draft/review.
6. Generate the diary video.
7. Show the final result and share/download button.

## Ideal Video Format

Primary format: 9:16 vertical MP4, 20-35 seconds.

Secondary format: 1:1 cropped version for the LinkedIn feed.

Optional format: 16:9 desktop/browser version only for technical posts.

## Recording Advice

- Record the actual app on a phone if possible.
- Use a neutral background or cover the camera with something intentional if privacy matters.
- Do not show personal footage unless it is deliberately staged.
- Keep taps visible and pacing fast.
- Add captions/text overlays in editing, not inside the app.

## Video Script

- 0-3s: "IdleDiary: a tiny private video diary"
- 3-8s: "Capture one 3-second moment"
- 8-14s: "Review today's clips"
- 14-24s: "Generate a short vertical diary"
- 24-30s: "Private on your device unless you export"

## Demo Recommendation

Use real phone screen recording as the main demo.

For this project, a polished trailer is less important than trust. The product depends on phone camera behavior, local/private framing, and a quick capture flow. A real mobile recording proves that the app actually works where users will use it.

Best setup:

- Use iPhone or Android screen recording.
- Open the deployed production URL.
- Use staged footage: desk, sky, coffee, keyboard, notebook, anything non-private.
- Record the app flow in one take, then trim it down.
- Add simple captions afterward with CapCut, Screen Studio, iMovie, or Canva.
- Export as vertical MP4.
